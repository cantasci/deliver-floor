# Rating notch helper

Requirements: /Users/cantasci/Desktop/Projects/side-projects/skills-shop/examples/watchlist-poc/JOB-idea.md

Rating notch helper for the Watchlist tool

Our credit analysts work out by hand how many notches a client's or a country's rating has moved and what Watchlist level
that gives. We would like the system to do it for them: the notch calculator and the country rating change indicator from
the POC (docs/POC_Requirements_v0.2_EN.md in the repo). Other parts of the POC will use it; this is not about screens.
